Core Idea

One formula to understand all emotions

⚖️

Emotion = Expectation × Validation

You are constantly forming expectations about the world. When reality (or thought) validates an expectation, you feel pleasure. When it violates one, you feel pain — all emotions can be traced back to specific expectations.

1

Identify Trigger

What event, conversation, or thought triggered the emotional response?

2

Trace Expectation

What did you expect to happen? Make it explicit.

3

Check Validation

Was the expectation validated (pleasure) or violated (pain)?

4

Reframe

Is this expectation realistic? What expectation would serve you better?
